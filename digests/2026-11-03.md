## PM Concept of the Day: Stakeholder Engagement Assessment Matrix

The Stakeholder Engagement Assessment Matrix is a PMBOK tool that compares each stakeholder's current engagement level with the level the project needs. The levels are Unaware, Resistant, Neutral, Supportive and Leading, usually marked C (current) and D (desired) in a grid. Any gap between C and D becomes an action in the stakeholder engagement plan, such as more communication, a one-to-one conversation or a change in how they are involved. In a fintech core-banking migration, the compliance officer might be Neutral when you need them Supportive, and a gap like that is how sign-off delays happen late in the project. For the exam, remember it is a different tool from the Power/Interest grid: that one decides who to prioritise, while this one tracks how engaged they are against the target.

## One Thing to Try Today (10 minutes)

List five stakeholders on a project you know (real or imagined, such as launching a new payment method). Draw a quick C/D grid and mark each person's current and desired engagement. For every gap, write one specific action to close it, such as "30-minute walkthrough with the fraud lead before sprint review".
