## PM Concept of the Day: Benefits Realization Management

Benefits Realization Management is the practice of defining, tracking, and confirming the business benefits a project was meant to deliver, not just whether it shipped on time and on budget. A benefits register names each expected benefit (e.g., 15% lower cost-to-serve), an owner, a measure, a baseline, and a target date, and it continues after project closure because most benefits only appear once the product is in use. It appears on the PMP exam under business value and governance: a project can finish on schedule and still fail if the benefits never materialize. Valley National's Bluevine deal is a good example: the acquisition's "done" milestone is closing, but the real benefits (small-business deposit growth, lower customer acquisition cost) only show up after the platform integration, so a PM would assign a benefit owner and track those metrics for quarters after go-live.

## One Thing to Try Today

Pick a recent fintech acquisition (Valley/Bluevine works) and spend 10 minutes writing a mini benefits register: three expected benefits, a measurable KPI for each, and who would own it. Keep it as a reusable template for your portfolio.
