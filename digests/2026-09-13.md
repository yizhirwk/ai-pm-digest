## PM Concept of the Day: INVEST Criteria for User Stories

INVEST is a mnemonic checklist for writing good agile user stories: **I**ndependent, **N**egotiable, **V**aluable, **E**stimable, **S**mall, and **T**estable. A story that fails one of these criteria usually causes downstream pain — for example, a story that isn't Independent creates sequencing deadlocks in sprint planning, and one that isn't Testable can't be verified against a Definition of Done. In fintech IT delivery, a story like "add support for real-time payment status webhooks" often needs splitting to satisfy Small and Independent — separating the webhook delivery mechanism from the retry/dead-letter handling — so each piece can be estimated, built, and tested without blocking the other.

## One Thing to Try Today

Pick one user story currently sitting in your backlog and run it through the INVEST checklist letter by letter. If it fails on two or more criteria (commonly Small or Testable), spend 10 minutes rewriting or splitting it before it reaches sprint planning.
