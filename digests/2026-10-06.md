## PM Concept of the Day: Velocity and Capacity Planning

Velocity is the amount of work (usually story points) a Scrum team completes in a sprint, averaged over the last several sprints to forecast how much it can take on next. Capacity planning adjusts that forecast for known factors such as holidays, leave, and on-call or support load. Together they let a PM turn a backlog into a realistic release forecast instead of a hopeful date, and they are common Agile exam topics. Take the PSD3 rules above: a bank team with a 21-month runway could divide the estimated points for name-to-IBAN checks and the permission dashboard by its trailing velocity to see whether it fits. Velocity is a planning tool, not a performance target, since pressuring teams to raise it tends to inflate estimates rather than speed up delivery.

## One Thing to Try Today (10 min)

Pick a small personal or work project with a list of tasks. Estimate each task in points, note how many you finish in a week, and use that number to forecast when the rest will be done. Write down how far off the forecast turns out to be next week.
