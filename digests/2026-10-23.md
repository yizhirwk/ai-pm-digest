# Daily PM + Fintech Digest — 2026-10-23

## Fintech News

_Note: search surfaced limited same-day coverage, so items 1–3 are from Oct 21–22 and Oct 1, and item 4 is from the past week._

1. **OCC issues its final rule implementing the GENIUS Act stablecoin framework (Oct 21).** The rule bars stablecoin issuers from paying yield directly to customers, though the OCC says it will assess third-party yield arrangements case by case. It also sets reserve and structural requirements for national banks and other covered issuers, with the rule and the Act taking effect January 18, 2027. ([Banking Dive](https://www.bankingdive.com/news/occ-final-rule-genius-stablecoin-yield-gould-bessent-icba/833638/), [Payment Expert](https://paymentexpert.com/2026/10/22/trum-praises-genius-act-final-rule/))
2. **UK FCA publishes final rules for its cryptoasset regime.** Trading platforms, intermediaries, custodians, stablecoin issuers and staking firms will all need FCA authorisation, with the application window opening 30 September 2026 and go-live in October 2027. ([Bratby Law](https://bratby.law/fca-work-programme-2026-payments-growth-financial-crime/), [PYMNTS](https://www.pymnts.com/cryptocurrency/2026/this-week-in-stablecoins-fintech-big-tech-and-regulation))
3. **Fiserv's digital-currency hosting platform goes live.** North Dakota's state stablecoin is the first digital asset on the system, a sign that core processors are turning stablecoin support into a standard bank-facing service. ([Banking Dive](https://www.bankingdive.com/topic/fintech/))

## PM Concept of the Day: Now-Next-Later Roadmap

A Now-Next-Later roadmap groups work by certainty rather than by calendar dates: "Now" is committed and in progress, "Next" is well understood and queued, and "Later" is an explored but unscheduled idea. It avoids the false precision of date-based roadmaps, where a slipped quarter looks like a broken promise, and it makes stakeholders discuss outcomes and priorities instead of deadlines. In fintech, a roadmap might put "OCC stablecoin rule gap analysis" in Now, "reserve reporting changes" in Next, and "yield-product options" in Later, so the team stays honest about regulatory uncertainty while still showing direction. For the exam, connect it to progressive elaboration and adaptive planning: detail increases as an item moves closer to Now.
