## PM Concept of the Day: Sprint Review

The Sprint Review is the Scrum event at the end of each Sprint where the Scrum Team and stakeholders inspect the Increment and adapt the Product Backlog based on what they see. It is a working session, not a status-report slide deck: the team demonstrates what is actually done against the Definition of Done, stakeholders give feedback, and the group discusses what has changed in the market, budget, or timeline before deciding what to build next. It is often confused with the Sprint Retrospective, which looks inward at the team's process, while the Review looks outward at the product. In fintech delivery, a Review for a dispute-management feature might have operations and compliance staff click through a real chargeback flow in staging and flag a missing audit-trail field, which is far cheaper to catch in a two-week Sprint than after a regulatory exam. For PMP and Agile exams, remember that it is time-boxed (max four hours for a one-month Sprint) and that its output is an updated, re-prioritized backlog.
