## PM Concept of the Day: Communications Management Plan

A communications management plan defines who needs what information, how often, in what format, and through which channel, so that information reaches stakeholders deliberately rather than by accident. A classic PMP exam point is that the number of communication channels is n(n-1)/2, so a team of 5 has 10 channels and a team of 10 has 45, which is why adding people makes coordination harder. In fintech delivery, the plan might specify a weekly status note to the sponsor, a daily standup for engineers, and a formal, documented update to the sponsor bank's compliance contact before each release. It should be revisited whenever stakeholders change, such as when a new regulator, vendor, or partner bank joins the project.

## One Thing to Try Today

Pick a project you know and spend 10 minutes drafting a mini communications table with four columns: stakeholder, what they need, frequency, and channel. List at least five stakeholders, then circle the one who is currently getting the least useful updates.
