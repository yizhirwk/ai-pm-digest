## PM Concept of the Day: Technical Debt Management

Technical debt is the accumulated cost of shortcuts taken in design or code that make future change slower and riskier, like interest on a loan. For a PM, the key is making it visible and deliberate: log debt items in the backlog, size them, and reserve a fixed share of capacity (often 10-20%) so they are paid down instead of silently eroding velocity. In fintech this is especially pointed, since a rushed integration with a payments processor or core banking system can later block compliance changes or cause reconciliation errors. Agile and PMP-style quality discussions treat this as a trade-off to document with the sponsor, not a purely engineering matter.

## One Thing to Try Today (10 min)

Pick any project you know (or a public app) and write down three plausible "shortcuts" a team might take to hit a deadline. For each, note the future cost it creates and how you would make it visible in a backlog (a ticket title plus a one-line impact statement).
