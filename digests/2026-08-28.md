- **Revolut is launching a euro-denominated stablecoin on Ethereum.** The 80-million-customer neobank is pushing further into digital assets, joining a wave of consumer fintechs building their own stablecoin rails rather than just supporting third-party tokens — a sign that stablecoin issuance is becoming a competitive feature, not just a crypto-native niche.
- **Klarna's Q2 results show its growth is now translating into real profitability.** The buy-now-pay-later giant reported $1.042 billion in revenue (up 27% year-over-year), GMV of $36.6 billion, and operating income of $27 million — a sharp swing from a $46 million loss a year earlier — reinforcing that post-IPO scrutiny is pushing large consumer fintechs to prioritize unit economics over pure growth.

## PM Concept of the Day: Risk Response Strategies (Avoid, Mitigate, Transfer, Accept, Escalate)

Once a risk has been identified and logged — the job of a risk register — PMP and Agile practice both call for choosing a deliberate *response* strategy rather than reacting ad hoc: **avoid** (change the plan to eliminate the risk entirely), **mitigate** (reduce its probability or impact), **transfer** (shift ownership, often contractually, to a third party better positioned to bear it), **accept** (acknowledge it and do nothing further, sometimes with a contingency reserve), or **escalate** (hand it to a level of governance beyond the project's authority, common when the risk is regulatory or organizational rather than technical). This is a distinct exam topic from simply identifying and prioritizing risks in a register — it's about what you *do* once a risk is understood. Today's news is a clean illustration: a fintech PM shipping a stablecoin feature might **transfer** compliance risk to a sponsor bank partnership, **mitigate** regulatory uncertainty by building configurable jurisdiction controls ahead of the OCC's November rule, or **escalate** an unresolved question about UK DLT rules to legal and executive sponsors rather than deciding it at the project level. Choosing the wrong strategy — accepting a risk that should have been escalated — is exactly the kind of judgment PMP scenario questions test.

## One Thing to Try Today

Pick one regulatory item from today's news (the OCC's incoming GENIUS Act rule, or the UK's new DLT mandate for the Bank of England) and spend 10-15 minutes writing one sentence for each of the five risk response strategies — avoid, mitigate, transfer, accept, escalate — describing what a concrete action would look like if you were the PM for a fintech product affected by that rule. The goal is building the reflex to see a single risk through all five lenses before picking one.
