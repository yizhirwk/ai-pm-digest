## PM Concept of the Day: Contingency Reserve vs. Management Reserve

A project budget has two different safety buffers. The *contingency reserve* covers identified risks (the "known unknowns"). It sits inside the cost baseline and the PM can spend it, usually according to the risk response plan. The *management reserve* covers unforeseen work (the "unknown unknowns"). It sits outside the cost baseline but within the total project budget, and spending it needs sponsor or management approval, which also changes the baseline. Say a fintech team is integrating a card-issuing processor. It holds a contingency reserve for the identified risk that the vendor's sandbox is delayed. If a regulator then issues new data-residency rules mid-project, that rework is funded from the management reserve. On the exam, remember: baseline plus management reserve equals the project budget.

## One Thing to Try Today (10 minutes)

Take a project you know, or a hypothetical one such as a payments-API launch, and list its top five risks. Put a rough dollar or day estimate on each, then multiply by its probability. The sum is a first-cut contingency reserve. Then write one sentence on what kind of event would have to happen to justify asking for management reserve instead.
