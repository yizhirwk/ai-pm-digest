## PM Concept of the Day: Lessons Learned Register

A lessons learned register is a living log of what went well, what went wrong and what to change. It is captured throughout the project, not only at closing. In PMP terms it is an output of Manage Project Knowledge and an input to Close Project, and it feeds the organizational process assets (OPAs) that future projects draw on. Each entry should state the situation, the impact, the root cause and a concrete recommendation with an owner. In fintech delivery, a payments-gateway cutover that slipped because the sponsor bank's certification queue was underestimated should become an entry such as "request certification slots in week 1." That way the next BaaS integration inherits the fix. The same applies to a regulatory deadline like the GENIUS Act rules above, where the first compliance build is the one the second build learns from.

## One Thing to Try Today (10 minutes)

Pick the Treasury stablecoin proposal (or any one regulatory notice from today's news) and write three bullets: what it requires, who inside a bank or fintech would have to act, and which delivery milestone it would put at risk. Practicing this regulation-to-roadmap translation is a core skill for a fintech PM.
