- **The Fed advances "skinny" master accounts for nonbanks.** The Federal Reserve's proposed limited-scope master account — nicknamed the "skinny" account by Governor Waller — would give fintechs and payment innovators direct, expedited (90-day) access to Fedwire, FedNow, and Fedwire Securities, without ACH, discount-window, or interest-bearing privileges, and subject to a balance cap of the lesser of $500M or 10% of the holder's assets. ## PM Concept of the Day: OKRs (Objectives and Key Results)

OKRs pair one qualitative **Objective** (what you want to achieve, and why it matters) with 2-4 measurable **Key Results** (how you'll know you got there) — distinct from a task list because Key Results describe outcomes, not activities. Unlike EVM or a WBS, which track whether planned work got done, OKRs force a team to define what success actually looks like before work starts. In fintech IT delivery, an objective like "make instant payouts reliable at scale" might carry key results such as "cut payout failure rate from 2% to 0.3%" and "cut P95 payout latency to under 5 seconds" — metrics that stay meaningful even if the underlying implementation plan changes mid-quarter.

## One Thing to Try Today

Take one initiative you're currently driving and write it as a single Objective with 2-3 measurable Key Results (not tasks — actual numbers you could report a "70% achieved" against). If you can't state a Key Result as a number or a clear yes/no, that's a sign the initiative's success criteria are still fuzzy.
