## PM Concept of the Day: RACI Matrix

A RACI matrix assigns each task or decision one **Responsible** party (does the work), one **Accountable** party (owns the outcome and signs off — only one per item), and any number of **Consulted** (give input beforehand) or **Informed** (told after the fact) stakeholders. It's a core PMP tool for clarifying ownership on cross-functional work where roles blur. In fintech IT delivery, a payments feature launch might have engineering as Responsible, the product lead as Accountable, compliance and risk as Consulted on regulatory requirements, and customer support as Informed before go-live — without this clarity, compliance sign-off is exactly the kind of step that gets missed under deadline pressure.

## One Thing to Try Today

Pick one feature or workstream you're currently involved in and draft a quick RACI matrix for it — just four columns and the key decisions/tasks. Notice whether any task has zero or multiple "Accountable" owners; that ambiguity is usually where fintech projects stall waiting on a sign-off nobody realizes they're blocking.
