## PM Concept of the Day: DORA Metrics (DevOps Research and Assessment)

DORA metrics are four measures — deployment frequency, lead time for changes, change failure rate, and mean time to restore (MTTR) — that DevOps research found correlate with high-performing software delivery teams. Unlike vanity metrics (lines of code, hours logged), they measure outcomes: how fast you ship, and how safely. For a fintech PM, these metrics are the practical way to prove that speed and compliance aren't opposites — a payments team with weekly deploys, a low change-failure rate, and a fast MTTR can move quickly *and* satisfy an auditor asking how incidents get caught and reversed. They're also a natural lens for exactly the kind of tension in today's news: a regtech firm adding AI-driven compliance checks to its pipeline needs to watch whether those checks are slowing lead time or catching real defects before release.

## One Thing to Try Today

Pick one delivery team you support and ask (or look up) just one number: how long does it currently take from "code merged" to "in production" for a typical change? Don't try to fix it yet — just get the real figure. That single lead-time number is the seed of a DORA baseline, and most PMs are surprised by how far off their mental estimate is from reality.
