# AI/PM + Fintech Digest — 2026-09-20

## Fintech Industry News

- **Tabby raises $233M at a $6.5B valuation.** The Saudi Arabia/UAE-based buy-now-pay-later and financial services provider closed a new equity round led by existing backer Blue Pool Capital, the single largest fintech raise of the past week and a signal that BNPL consolidation in the Gulf is accelerating rather than slowing down.
- **EU's PSD3/PSR package nears final publication.** The Third Payment Services Directive and its companion Payment Services Regulation are moving toward the Official Journal, the last procedural step before an 18-month transition clock starts running for every payment service provider operating in the bloc — a major compliance deadline PMs at EU-facing fintechs should start planning against now.
- **G20 backs clearer digital-asset rules but sidesteps stablecoins.** Finance ministers and central bank governors endorsed tighter regulatory and supervisory frameworks for digital assets generally, but stablecoins were explicitly excluded from that commitment while the Financial Stability Board continues reviewing global stablecoin arrangements — a reminder that cross-border stablecoin rules remain fragmented even as national regimes (US, EU, UK, Hong Kong, Singapore) mature individually.
- **Fed proposes first major Regulation O overhaul in decades.** The Federal Reserve Board's proposal would modernize the insider-lending rules that apply to all US banks, part of a broader deregulatory push that also includes the House's newly passed Main Street Capital Access Act aimed at easing de novo bank chartering.
- **Stablecoin infrastructure keeps attracting strategic capital.** Velocity, a stablecoin payments and treasury platform, closed a $10M Series A extension backed by Visa Ventures, Circle Ventures, and Ripple among others — another sign that incumbents are placing infrastructure bets on stablecoin rails rather than waiting for global rules to fully settle.

## PM Concept of the Day: Value Stream Mapping (VSM)

Value Stream Mapping is a Lean technique for visualizing every step a piece of work takes from request to delivery, explicitly separating time spent on actual work ("touch time") from time spent waiting in a queue between owners. In most software and fintech delivery pipelines, queue time dwarfs touch time — a compliance review or a vendor API approval can sit untouched for days while the "work" itself takes an hour. For a PM at a payments or banking-as-a-service company, VSM is especially useful for surfacing exactly this: with EU's PSD3 transition clock starting soon, mapping the value stream from "regulatory requirement identified" to "control implemented and audited" can reveal which handoffs (legal review, engineering estimation, QA sign-off) are the true bottleneck long before the deadline arrives. Unlike a generic process diagram, VSM forces you to attach a number — lead time and touch time — to every step, turning "things feel slow" into a specific, actionable target for improvement.

## One Thing to Try Today

Pick one recurring handoff in your own delivery process — for example, a ticket moving from "dev complete" to "QA sign-off," or a vendor integration moving from "contract signed" to "API access granted." Spend 10 minutes sketching it as a simple value stream: list each step, and next to each one write your best estimate of the touch time (actual work) versus the wait time (sitting in a queue). You'll likely find the wait time is 3-5x the touch time — that gap is your highest-leverage target for process improvement, and now you have a concrete number to bring to your next retro or standup.
