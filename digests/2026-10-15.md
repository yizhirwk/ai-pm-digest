## PM Concept of the Day: MVP vs. MMP (Minimum Viable vs. Minimum Marketable Product)

A Minimum Viable Product is the smallest release that lets a team test a key hypothesis with real users and learn from it. A Minimum Marketable Product is the smallest release that customers would actually pay for or that the business can responsibly launch. The two are easy to confuse, and Agile exam questions often hinge on that difference: an MVP optimizes for learning, an MMP for market value. In fintech the gap is wider because regulation sets a floor. A new peer-to-peer payments feature might be tested as an MVP with a small pilot group, but a public launch (the MMP) also needs KYC/AML checks, dispute handling, and audit logging before it can ship.

## One Thing to Try Today

Pick a feature you use or are building and spend 10 minutes writing two one-line scope statements: what the MVP would test, and what extra the MMP needs (compliance, support, reliability). Note which items on the MMP list are non-negotiable and why.
