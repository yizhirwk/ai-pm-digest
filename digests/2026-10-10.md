## PM Concept of the Day: Integrated Change Control (Change Control Board)

Integrated Change Control is the PMBOK process of reviewing every change request, assessing its impact on scope, schedule, cost, quality and risk, and then approving, rejecting or deferring it through a defined authority such as a Change Control Board (CCB). Approved changes update the baselines and the change log, so the project always has one agreed version of the truth. On the exam, remember that the PM should assess impact first and never implement a change before approval. In fintech delivery, a late regulator-driven requirement, such as one of this month's compliance deadlines, is a classic case: the CCB weighs the mandatory change against the delay it causes and re-baselines the plan.

## One Thing to Try Today (10 min)

Write a one-page change request template with these fields: description, reason, impact on scope/schedule/cost/risk, options, and approver. Then fill it in for an imagined late requirement, such as "add a second card network routing option," and note who would sit on the CCB.
