- **UK FCA cryptoasset application window opens today (30 Sept).** Firms can now apply for authorisation under the UK's new cryptoasset regime; the window is expected to run to 28 Feb 2027, with the regime becoming mandatory on 25 Oct 2027. Firms that apply in the window are expected to get a smoother transition and keep their full permissions, while later applicants may face narrower ones. (Dates are as reported by law-firm commentary; check the FCA site for confirmation.)

## PM Concept of the Day: Product Backlog Refinement

Product Backlog Refinement is the ongoing Scrum activity of adding detail, estimates, and order to backlog items so the top of the backlog is ready for sprint planning. It's not a formal event in the Scrum Guide; it's continuous, and teams commonly spend around 10% of capacity on it. Items near the top should be small and clear enough to be pulled into a sprint, while items further down can stay coarse. In fintech delivery, this is where a story like "support FCA-registered crypto customers" gets split into KYC changes, disclosure copy, and reporting, with compliance questions raised before the sprint rather than mid-sprint. For PMP/PSM exams, remember that the Product Owner is accountable for the backlog, but the whole Scrum Team collaborates on refinement.

## One Thing to Try Today

Pick one regulatory headline from above and spend 10 minutes breaking it into 4-5 rough user stories, then order them by risk and dependency. Practising turning regulation into a refined, sequenced backlog is a core fintech PM skill.
