# PM + Fintech Digest — 2026-10-17

## Fintech News

1. **OCC fines American Express National Bank $350M over AML failures.** The regulator found the bank lacked a BSA/AML program reasonably designed to monitor compliance, citing weak staffing, controls, testing and training, with about $13B in suspected trade-based money laundering processed from 2014 to 2025. The Fed issued a separate cease-and-desist order the same day, and this is the OCC's first bank penalty since TD's $450M in October 2024. (Announced Oct 8; [OCC](https://occ.gov/news-issuances/news-releases/2026/nr-occ-2026-87.html), [Banking Dive](https://www.bankingdive.com/news/amex-dinged-by-fed-occ-must-pay-350m-for-aml-failures/832570/))

2. **Kraken's parent Payward agrees to buy Saldo Finance Oy, owner of Lithuania-licensed Saldo Bank.** Owning an EU-licensed bank would let Payward offer deposits, lending, cards and payments in Europe directly rather than through partners. No price was disclosed, and closing in 2027 depends on regulatory approval. (Announced Oct 14; [Finance Magnates](https://www.financemagnates.com/institutional-forex/kraken-parent-payward-to-acquire-european-bank/))

3. **Behavox acquires Zurich-based Apiax to consolidate compliance tooling.** Apiax turns regulations into machine-readable rules that run inside a firm's own systems, and Behavox plans to pair it with its regulatory-change and surveillance products in one evidence trail. It signals a push to replace several separate compliance vendors with one platform; terms weren't disclosed. (Announced about Oct 8; [FinTech Global](https://fintech.global/?p=259946), [FF News](https://ffnews.com/news/behavox-acquires-apiax-extending-its-controls-platform-into-cross-border-and-con-91b5969f))

4. **Customers Bank is adding Palantir AI to deposit onboarding.** The bank is using Palantir's AI to speed up how new deposit accounts are opened, another example of established banks applying AI to core operational workflows. Details beyond the headline were limited. (Reported Oct 16; [Hipther fintech brief](https://hipther.com/news/2026/10/16/161178/fintech-pulse-your-daily-industry-brief-october-16-2026-10x-banking-tungsten-automation-customers-ba))

## PM Concept of the Day: Phase Gates (Stage-Gate Reviews)

A phase gate is a formal checkpoint at the end of a project phase where a review body decides whether to proceed, proceed with conditions, rework, or stop, based on pre-agreed exit criteria. It matters for PMP and predictive delivery because it forces go/no-go decisions on evidence instead of momentum, and it limits spend on projects that no longer make sense. In fintech, gates often map to control sign-offs: a design gate requiring a completed AML/KYC risk assessment, a pre-launch gate requiring independent testing and compliance approval. The AmEx finding above, which cites weak independent testing and an assessment that underweighted its card businesses, is the kind of gap a well-defined gate catches by refusing to pass until the control's evidence exists.

## One Thing to Try Today

Pick a project you know (work or personal) and spend 10 minutes writing a 5-line gate checklist for its next milestone: three objective exit criteria, who decides, and what happens if a criterion fails. Check that each criterion is binary, a yes or no that someone can verify.
