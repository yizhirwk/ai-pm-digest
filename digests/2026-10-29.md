- **OCC finalizes its GENIUS Act stablecoin rule (issued Oct 21, 2026; effective Jan 18, 2027).** It is the first federal banking regulator to finalize rules for payment stablecoin issuers. It keeps the 1:1 reserve requirement and the ban on yield for simply holding a stablecoin, but drops a proposed presumption covering affiliate and third-party yield arrangements and relaxes some liquidity and redemption provisions. The FDIC and Federal Reserve have not yet finalized theirs, so teams building on stablecoin rails now have a concrete compliance baseline from one regulator and a wait on the others. ## PM Concept of the Day: User Acceptance Testing (UAT)

User Acceptance Testing is the final validation phase where the business users or customer representatives confirm that the delivered solution meets the acceptance criteria and works in real-world scenarios, as opposed to QA, which checks that it was built to specification. In PMP terms it is part of Validate Scope: the sponsor or customer formally accepts deliverables, and acceptance feeds into closing. In Agile it happens continuously, with the Product Owner accepting stories at the Sprint Review, but regulated work often adds a formal UAT window before release. In fintech, UAT might mean operations and compliance staff running real payment-reconciliation scenarios (refunds, chargebacks, failed settlements) in a staging environment before a go-live. The PM's job is to schedule UAT early, define entry and exit criteria, and make sure testers are available, because UAT is where late scope surprises surface.

## One Thing to Try Today (10 minutes)

Pick a feature you use regularly, such as a bank app's transfer flow, and write five UAT scenarios for it in Given-When-Then form. Include at least one failure case (insufficient funds, timeout) and define the pass/fail result for each. Save them in a note as a starter portfolio piece.
