## PM Concept of the Day: Communications Management Plan & Communication Channels

A communications management plan defines who needs what information, in what format, how often, and through which channel. The PMP exam also tests the channel formula n(n−1)/2: a team of 5 has 10 communication paths, but add 5 more people and it jumps to 45, which is why adding staff to a project can slow it down. In fintech delivery this shows up with regulated programs like a charter application: engineering, legal, compliance, the sponsor and the regulator all need different updates, so a PM sets up a weekly status for sponsors, a formal log for regulator correspondence, and a single decision channel instead of letting everyone talk to everyone. The plan keeps legal commitments and technical status from drifting apart.

## One Thing to Try Today (10 min)

Pick a project you know (or a hypothetical charter application) and list its 6-8 stakeholders. Compute the channel count with n(n−1)/2, then draft a 4-row mini communications plan: audience, information, frequency, channel.
