# PM + Fintech Digest — 2026-09-10

## Fintech Industry News

- **WealthTech mega-round leads a $1.3bn funding week.** FNZ, a global wealth management infrastructure platform, secured $450m in new equity funding as part of a broader week that saw over $1.3bn raised across 12 fintech deals, with wealthtech, payments infrastructure, and cybertech drawing the bulk of investor interest.
- **Singapore's MAS moves to tighten stablecoin rules.** The Monetary Authority of Singapore opened a public consultation on amendments to the Payment Services Act that would ban paying interest on regulated stablecoins and require issuers to run mandatory stress tests and maintain recovery and wind-down plans.
- **US Treasury clarifies stablecoin issuance rules.** The Treasury Department proposed rules defining what legally counts as issuing, offering, or selling a payment stablecoin in the US, and is seeking public input on how the new federal stablecoin law (under the GENIUS Act) should apply to cross-border activity.
- **Fed signals nonbank access to payment rails is coming.** Federal Reserve Governor Christopher Waller said he wants Fed payment accounts "operationalized" by the end of 2026, a move that would give nonbank payment companies more direct access to core payment infrastructure instead of routing through sponsor banks.
- **UK brings BNPL fully under regulatory supervision.** As of July 15, 2026, lenders offering deferred payment credit (the standard BNPL structure) to finance merchant purchases are now regulated by the FCA, closing a long-standing exemption and forcing BNPL providers into the same conduct and affordability rules as other consumer credit.

## PM Concept of the Day: Earned Value Management (EVM)

Earned Value Management is a PMP-tested technique for measuring project performance by comparing three numbers at any point in time: planned value (what work should be done), earned value (what work actually got done, valued at budget), and actual cost (what was really spent). From these, two ratios drive decisions — Schedule Performance Index (SPI = EV/PV) and Cost Performance Index (CPI = EV/AC) — with anything below 1.0 signaling the project is behind schedule or over budget. In a fintech IT delivery context, EVM is especially useful for regulatory-driven programs (like a KYC platform rebuild or a core banking migration) where sponsors need an objective, auditable answer to "are we on track" rather than a subjective status-report color. The discipline of EVM also forces PMs to define a credible baseline and a clear "definition of earned" per work package up front, which prevents the common trap of reporting percent-complete based on gut feel.

## One Thing to Try Today

Pick one in-flight task or ticket from your current sprint and estimate its Planned Value in dollars or hours (based on the budget or estimate allocated to it), then compare it against what's actually been spent or logged so far. Spend 10 minutes calculating a rough CPI for that single task — this small exercise builds the muscle for reading EVM dashboards and spotting cost overruns before they show up in a monthly report.
