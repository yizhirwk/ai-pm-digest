# PM + Fintech Daily Digest — 2026-08-24

## Fintech News

- **OCC grants Trump-linked World Liberty Financial preliminary approval for a national trust bank charter.** The proposed World Liberty Trust Company would take over issuance, reserve management, and custody for the USD1 stablecoin (now over $4B in circulation) from current issuer BitGo — a high-profile test of how comfortable federal regulators are chartering crypto-native firms directly as banks under the GENIUS Act era.

## PM Concept of the Day: The Iron Triangle (Triple Constraint)

The Iron Triangle is the foundational PMP model that a project's **scope**, **schedule**, and **cost** are mutually constraining — pull on one and at least one of the other two (or quality, often drawn as a fourth dimension in the middle) has to move. A PM's core job in planning is deciding up front which side is fixed, which can flex, and which is the release valve when reality doesn't cooperate. MoneySuperMarket's business-banking launch above is a clean illustration: rather than building regulated core-banking infrastructure themselves (which would blow out cost and schedule), they partnered with Countingup and Griffin to shrink their own scope to the front-end product, compressing time-to-market at the cost of direct control over the banking engine underneath. Any fintech PM evaluating "build vs. partner vs. buy" is really running an Iron Triangle trade-off, whether or not they name it that on the exam.

## One Thing to Try Today

Pick one active project or initiative and write down its scope, schedule, and cost/budget in one line each. Circle which one is truly fixed (a regulatory deadline, a hard budget cap, a non-negotiable feature) and which one is actually your flex point when something slips — if you can't name the flex point in under 5 minutes, that's a sign the trade-off hasn't been made explicit with your stakeholders yet.
