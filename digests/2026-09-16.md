## PM Concept of the Day: Progressive Delivery (Canary Releases & Feature Flags)

Progressive delivery extends continuous delivery by decoupling code deployment from feature release: new functionality ships to production behind a feature flag, then gets exposed gradually — to internal users, a small canary percentage, then everyone — while the team watches error rates and business metrics at each step. If something breaks, the team flips the flag off instead of executing a full rollback. For a PM this reframes risk management: instead of a single high-stakes go/no-go release decision, risk is spread across many small, reversible exposure increases. In fintech and regulated software delivery, this is especially valuable because it lets teams validate a new payment flow or KYC change against real transaction volume without exposing every customer at once, and it gives compliance and risk teams a natural checkpoint to review data before full rollout. PMP and Agile exam questions on this topic usually test whether a candidate understands that decoupling deployment from release reduces blast radius without slowing down delivery cadence.

## One Thing to Try Today

Pick a feature you've seen roll out gradually in a banking or payments app you use (a redesigned checkout, a new transfer limit, a new fraud check). Spend 10 minutes sketching how you'd stage its exposure — what percentage of users would see it first, what metric would make you pause the rollout, and who (engineering, risk, compliance) would need to sign off before going to 100%. This builds the habit of thinking in rollout stages rather than single launch dates.
