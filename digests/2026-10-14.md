## PM Concept of the Day: Project Charter

A project charter is the short document that formally authorizes a project and gives the project manager authority to apply resources to it. It states the business case, high-level objectives and scope, key milestones, summary budget, major risks, success criteria, and the named sponsor. On the PMP exam it is the key output of Develop Project Charter in the Initiating process, and a common trap is that the PM does not own the charter. The sponsor or initiator issues it, and the PM usually helps draft it. In fintech delivery, a charter for a new instant-payments integration might name the compliance sign-off as a success criterion and cap the budget at the sponsor-approved figure, so later scope debates have an agreed reference point.

## One Thing to Try Today

Spend 10 minutes drafting a one-page charter for a project you know, such as a recent release or a side project. Write the objective, three success criteria, in-scope and out-of-scope lists, and the sponsor. Then check whether anyone on the real project would have disagreed with any line.
