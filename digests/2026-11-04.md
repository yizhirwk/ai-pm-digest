## PM Concept of the Day: Communication Channels Formula (n(n-1)/2)

The number of communication channels in a project is n(n-1)/2, where n is the number of people involved, and it grows much faster than headcount. A team of 5 has 10 channels, while a team of 10 has 45, so adding people raises coordination overhead sharply. This is why the Communications Management Plan defines who needs what information, how often, and through which channel, instead of leaving everyone to talk to everyone. In a fintech delivery, adding a bank partner, a compliance team and an outside auditor to a 10-person squad lifts it to 13 people and 78 channels, which is a common reason status information gets lost. On the exam, remember that a new stakeholder increases n by one, so recompute rather than just adding one channel.

## One Thing to Try Today (10 minutes)

Count the people on a project you know (real or imagined) and compute n(n-1)/2. Then write down the two or three channels that matter most, such as a weekly compliance sync or a shared Slack channel for the bank partner, and note one channel you could cut or replace with a shared dashboard.
