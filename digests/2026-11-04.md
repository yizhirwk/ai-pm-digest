## PM Concept of the Day: Earned Value Management (EVM)

Earned Value Management measures project performance by comparing three numbers: Planned Value (PV, the work scheduled to date), Earned Value (EV, the budgeted value of the work actually completed) and Actual Cost (AC). From them you get Schedule Performance Index (SPI = EV/PV) and Cost Performance Index (CPI = EV/AC); below 1.0 means behind schedule or over budget. Suppose a payments-gateway integration has $200K of work planned by month 3, only $150K of work is done, and you have spent $180K. Then SPI = 0.75 and CPI = 0.83, which tells you early that both the date and the budget are at risk. For the exam, remember that EAC = BAC/CPI is the forecast when current cost performance is expected to continue.

## One Thing to Try Today (10 minutes)

Take any small project, real or imagined, with a $100K budget and 10 equal tasks. Pretend 4 are done after spending $55K, with 5 planned by now. Calculate PV, EV, AC, SPI and CPI, then write one sentence you would say to a sponsor about the result.
