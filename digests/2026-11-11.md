## PM Concept of the Day: Management by Exception (Tolerances & Escalation)

Management by exception is a core PRINCE2 principle that also shows up in PMP governance questions. The sponsor or board sets **tolerances**, which are allowed ranges for time, cost, scope, quality, risk and benefits. The PM then runs the project inside those ranges without asking for approval at each step. When a forecast shows a tolerance will be breached, the PM raises an **exception report** that presents options, and the decision goes up a level. Management by exception is not a status report. In a fintech setting, a payments migration might carry tolerances of ±2 weeks and ±10% budget, plus zero tolerance on a PCI-DSS compliance slip. A one-week vendor delay stays with the PM. A forecast that the cutover will miss the card-network certification window gets escalated right away, with options such as adding parallel-run budget or descoping a market. Clear tolerances stop small decisions from piling up with the sponsor, and they stop large ones from being buried at team level.

## One Thing to Try Today (10 minutes)

Pick a project or feature you're close to, real or hypothetical. Write a three-row tolerance table for **schedule, budget and compliance/risk**. For each row, note the threshold that would trigger escalation and who it would go to. Then write one sentence describing a realistic event that would breach one tolerance, and list the two options you'd put in the exception report.
