## PM Concept of the Day: Scrum Accountabilities (Product Owner, Scrum Master, Developers)

Scrum defines three accountabilities on one Scrum Team: the Product Owner owns the value of the product and the ordering of the Product Backlog, the Scrum Master is accountable for the team's effectiveness and for removing impediments (a servant-leader, not a project manager), and the Developers own turning backlog items into a usable Increment each Sprint. The classic exam trap is mixing these up: the Product Owner decides *what* and *why*, the Developers decide *how* and *how much fits*, and the Scrum Master coaches the process but assigns no tasks. In fintech delivery, picture a stablecoin-compliance feature after a new rule like the OCC's: the PO ranks "reserve reporting" above "yield display" based on regulatory deadlines, the Developers size and design it, and the Scrum Master escalates a blocked dependency on the compliance team's sign-off. Watch for the anti-pattern where compliance or an executive dictates the sprint plan directly instead of working through the PO.
