## PM Concept of the Day: Tuckman's Stages of Team Development

Bruce Tuckman's model says teams typically move through **Forming** (polite, unclear roles), **Storming** (conflict over approach and ownership), **Norming** (agreed working rules), **Performing** (high, autonomous output), and later **Adjourning** (wrap-up). It is a PMP resource-management topic because a PM's leadership style should match the stage: directive when forming, facilitating through storming, and delegating once performing. Take HSBC's RedCoin launch above: a cross-functional team of treasury, compliance, engineering and legal staff will storm over things like reserve reconciliation ownership and launch gating, and a PM who expects that, rather than treating it as dysfunction, can resolve it with explicit working agreements. Note that adding a new member or a new regulator-driven scope shift can push a team back to an earlier stage, so velocity may dip temporarily.

## One Thing to Try Today (10 min)

Think of a team you're on (or were recently on) and write down which Tuckman stage it is in right now, with two observable behaviours as evidence. Then note one action a PM could take to move it forward, such as clarifying a role or writing down a working agreement.
