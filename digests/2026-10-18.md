## PM Concept of the Day: Tailoring and Hybrid Delivery Approaches

Tailoring means choosing and adapting predictive, agile, or hybrid practices to fit a project's context instead of applying one methodology everywhere. It is a core theme of the PMBOK 7 principles and the PMP exam, which often asks you to pick the right approach from scenario clues such as requirements volatility, regulatory constraints, and team maturity. In fintech, a common hybrid is to run the product build in two-week sprints while the compliance-sensitive parts, such as a KYC vendor integration or a bank-partner launch, follow phase gates and documented approvals. The skill is naming which parts of the work are uncertain, so they get iterative treatment, and which are fixed by regulators or contracts, so they get predictive control.

## One Thing to Try Today

Take a current or past project and spend 10 minutes splitting its work into two columns: "uncertain, so iterate" and "fixed, so plan up front." Then write one sentence on which delivery approach each column should use and why.
