## PM Concept of the Day: Pre-Mortem Analysis

A pre-mortem is a risk-identification technique where, before work starts, the team imagines the project has failed spectacularly and works backwards to list why. Framing failure as a given lets people voice doubts they would otherwise suppress, which counters optimism bias and groupthink. The output feeds the risk register with causes, owners and responses. In fintech, run one before a core-banking migration or a new payments launch: "It's six months later and settlement reconciliation broke on go-live. Why?" It often surfaces vendor API limits, untested edge cases or compliance sign-off delays early enough to plan for them.

## One Thing to Try Today (10 minutes)

Pick a project you know, or a public fintech launch such as BNPL providers adapting to FCA rules. Set a 5-minute timer and write 8 reasons it could fail. Then mark the top 3 by probability × impact and write one mitigation for each.
