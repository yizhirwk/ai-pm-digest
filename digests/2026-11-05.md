## PM Concept of the Day: Pareto Chart (80/20 Rule)

A Pareto chart is a quality tool that combines a descending bar chart of defect or issue causes with a cumulative-percentage line. It shows the "vital few" causes that produce most of the problems, based on the idea that roughly 80% of effects come from about 20% of causes. Teams use it to decide where to spend limited effort, instead of fixing everything equally. In fintech delivery, you might chart a month of failed payment transactions by cause. If three causes, such as timeouts with a bank API, duplicate-submission errors and invalid account formats, make up 75% of failures, those become the next sprint's priority. On the exam, remember that it is a quality-management tool, that the bars must be ordered from largest to smallest, and that it ranks causes by frequency, not by fix cost.

## One Thing to Try Today (10 minutes)

Take the last 20 bugs, support tickets or incidents from any project you know (or a public issue tracker). Tag each with a single cause category, tally them, and sort from largest to smallest. Write one sentence saying which top one or two categories you would tackle first and why.
