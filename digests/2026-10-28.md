## PM Concept of the Day: Team Charter and Working Agreements

A team charter is a document the team writes for itself, in contrast to the project charter that the sponsor issues. It records the team's values, ground rules, decision-making approach, communication norms, and conflict-resolution process. In Agile teams the day-to-day version is the **working agreement**, a short list the team owns and revisits in retrospectives, such as "PRs get a first review within 4 hours" or "no deploys on Friday afternoon". PMP treats this as a team-development tool: rules set early, with the whole team involved, cut friction as the team moves through Tuckman's forming and storming stages. In fintech delivery it matters most when engineering, compliance, and a bank partner all work together. For example, a working agreement that any change touching payment flows needs a compliance reviewer's sign-off within one business day turns a recurring source of delay into a predictable step.

## One Thing to Try Today (10 minutes)

Draft a five-line working agreement for a team you're on or a group project you know. Cover response time on reviews or messages, how decisions get made, and when to escalate a blocker. Put a number on each line (hours, days, people) so it can be checked, then save it where you'd bring it to the next retrospective.
