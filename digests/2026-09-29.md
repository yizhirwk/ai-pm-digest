# PM + Fintech Daily Digest — 2026-09-29

## Fintech News

- **Nubank reportedly weighs a bid for Monzo.** Monzo is said to be in early-stage sale talks with Brazil's Nu Holdings at a valuation reported at up to roughly £10bn, having hired Morgan Stanley and Qatalyst to run a process. Advent International has separately discussed a minority stake, which would reportedly only proceed if the Nubank talks stall. It would give Nubank a European foothold and dents the London IPO pipeline.
- **UK banks complete first live retail tokenised-deposit transactions.** Under UK Finance's Great British Tokenised Deposit (GBTD) initiative, banks including NatWest, Lloyds, HSBC UK, Barclays, Nationwide, Santander and Monzo ran live customer transactions (including remortgage completions and a marketplace escrow-style payment) on a Quant-built shared platform. Further pilots linking tokenised deposits to digital-asset settlement are expected in coming months.
- **LHV Bank puts agentic AI on customer email.** LHV rolled out an agentic AI system from Gradient Labs to handle customer emails (Sept 28), another sign that banks are moving from AI chatbot pilots to AI agents handling real servicing work.
- **Purple Group opens an embedded-finance division.** Through its Hello Purple business, the South African group is packaging its own financial technology as an offering for fintech developers — a small but typical example of firms productising internal platforms as BaaS.

## PM Concept of the Day: Sprint Retrospective

The Sprint Retrospective is the Scrum event at the end of each sprint where the team inspects how it worked — people, process, tools — and commits to concrete improvements, separate from the Sprint Review, which inspects the product. A common format is "what went well / what didn't / what will we change," but the exam-relevant point is that at least one improvement should be carried into the next sprint backlog so it actually gets done. In fintech delivery, a retro after a payments release might surface that late compliance sign-off repeatedly blocked stories; the action item could be inviting the compliance analyst to refinement so requirements are checked before the sprint starts. The Scrum Master facilitates and keeps it psychologically safe, and on the PMP/PSM exams the retro is about the team's process, not stakeholder feedback on the increment.

## One Thing to Try Today

Take the last meeting or work session you were part of and spend 10 minutes writing a mini-retro: one thing that went well, one friction point, and one specific, owner-assigned change you'd propose (e.g. "send the agenda 24h ahead"). Practicing turning vague frustration into a single testable improvement is the core habit of a delivery-focused PM.
