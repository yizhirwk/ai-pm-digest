## PM Concept of the Day: Root Cause Analysis (Fishbone Diagram & 5 Whys)

Root Cause Analysis is a quality-management technique for finding why a problem happened rather than patching its symptom. A Fishbone (Ishikawa) diagram groups possible causes into categories such as people, process, tools, environment, and vendors, while the 5 Whys technique asks "why?" repeatedly until it reaches a cause the team can actually fix. On the PMP exam these appear under Plan Quality Management and Manage Quality as basic quality tools, and the best answer usually fixes the cause, not the defect. In fintech delivery, if a payment reconciliation job fails every month-end, 5 Whys might trace it from "timeout" to "batch size" to "no load test on month-end volumes", which points to a fix in the test plan rather than a one-off rerun.

## One Thing to Try Today

Take one recurring problem from your current or past project (a late release, a flaky test, a missed deadline) and spend 10 minutes running a 5 Whys on it, writing each answer down. Circle the last "why" that is within the team's control and note one preventive action.
